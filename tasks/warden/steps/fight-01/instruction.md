# Warden

You are in a fight. The other fighter is the Warden.

1. Read `/app/world/RULES.md`.
2. Read `/app/view.txt`.
3. Past notes are in `/app/notes.md`. Read them if they exist.
4. Past session logs are under `/app/sessions/` — read them if useful.
5. Play with these commands until the view says you won or you died:

```text
warden status
warden attack
warden block
warden dodge left
warden dodge right
warden parry
warden jump
warden potion
```

Each command is one action. The Warden answers in the same command.
Keep playing. The Warden repeats its poses until you win or die.

6. Write what you learned in `/app/notes.md`.
7. Stop. Do not reset the fight.
