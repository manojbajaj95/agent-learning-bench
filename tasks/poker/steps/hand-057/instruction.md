# Poker

Read `/app/world/RULES.md` and `/app/view.txt`. Past session logs are under
`/app/sessions/` when available.

Play this hand with:

```text
poker status
poker act fold
poker act check
poker act call
poker act raise 30
```

`raise 30` means raise to 30 chips. `status` is free. Stop when the hand is over.
Do not try to start the next hand.
